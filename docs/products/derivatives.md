# Options

Private **options and forwards** for treasuries and market makers who need confidentiality on direction while keeping settlement verifiable.

## Status

**Live** on Sonic mainnet — open in the [app](https://app.aegisprotocol.org/derivatives).

- Create, exercise, and settle with Groth16 proofs (`derivative` circuit)
- Settlement uses the on-chain **AGS/SONIC** mark shown in the Options panel
- Pre-TGE the mark may be a governance bootstrap price; after AGS/wS liquidity, keepers can sync from the pool; listed Sonic oracles ([Chainlink, Pyth, API3, …](https://docs.soniclabs.com/sonic/build-on-sonic/tooling-and-infra#oracles)) plug in when an AGS feed exists

## What you can do

1. Connect a wallet on Sonic (chainId `146`).
2. Open **Options** in the app sidebar.
3. Confirm the settlement spot is fresh, then open a call or put with the trade panel.
