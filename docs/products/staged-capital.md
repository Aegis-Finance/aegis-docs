# Staged capital

**VC-style vaults** — capital tranches unlock when milestones are proven, not when a spreadsheet says so.

## Mechanics

- LPs commit to a vault with published unlock schedule
- Founders claim tranches against milestone proofs
- Unlock rules are fixed when the vault is created and enforced on-chain
