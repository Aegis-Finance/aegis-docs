# Lending

Borrow and lend with **ZK-backed collateral checks** — prove you meet tenor and LTV requirements without publishing your full balance sheet.

## Features

- Collateralized positions with on-chain liquidation rules
- Private credit profiles where deployed (Shielded+)
- Integration with shielded balances as collateral source

## Risk

Liquidations are automatic when health factors breach thresholds. Oracle and parameter risk is governance-controlled.
