# Insurance

On-chain **coverage pools** with programmable premiums and claims — rules enforced by contracts, not PDF policies.

## How it works

1. Create or join a pool with published parameters.
2. Pay premiums into the pool.
3. Submit claims with zero-knowledge proofs when required — prove eligibility without posting raw sensor or personal data on-chain.
