# Staking & yield

**Staking** locks AGS for protocol security and rewards. **Yield farming** distributes shielded incentive claims across pools and modules.

## Staking

- Private stake paths when enabled in the app
- Unbonding and reward rules are contract-defined

## Yield farming

- Earn rewards across eligible pools and modules
- Claims may stay shielded when that path is enabled
