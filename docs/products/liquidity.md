# Liquidity

Provide liquidity on **public constant-product pools** paired with the canonical swap router.

## What LPs see

- Pool reserves and share tokens on SonicScan
- Fees accrue per pool rules
- Impermanent loss behaves like standard AMM math

## Incentives

DAO-funded **liquidity mining** epochs may apply on selected pairs — see Treasury & LP module.
