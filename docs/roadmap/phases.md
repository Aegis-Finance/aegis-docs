# 2026–2030 phases

Where Aegis is headed after the Sonic DeFi core. Dates are targets, not guarantees — we ship when the product is ready, and we stay open to the right opportunities along the way.

## What we are building

Aegis starts as **private DeFi on Sonic**. From there we deepen the stack: more liquidity, better trading rails, stronger privacy defaults, and real payment surfaces that still respect selective disclosure.

We do **not** publish a full multi-year product calendar here. Surprises are part of the fun — watch [Community](../resources/community) for what ships next.

## Near term — protocol & markets

- Harden and expand modules already live on Sonic (wallet, swap, LP, lend, stake, insure, govern)
- Grow AGS markets, routing quality, and limit/RFQ flows — see [Trading & DEX](./trading)
- Keep verification, timelocked governance, and signed releases as the trust baseline

## Experimental — agents

**Agent-to-agent micro-commerce**

We are already toying with AI agents that hold wallets and pay each other for small jobs — early and experimental, but it points at an economy between algorithms, not only between people.

## Commerce surfaces

- ZK merchant paygate beta path — see [Commerce & partners](./ecosystem)
- Relayer competition for gas abstraction on privacy entry

## What we are not claiming yet

- That every roadmap idea is a finished product today
- That experimental agent payments are production-ready for everyone
- That “private” means zero metadata leakage on a public chain

*We move when security and product quality are green — and we take the opportunities that fit this path.*
