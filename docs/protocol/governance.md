# Governance

Protocol parameters change through **on-chain governance** — proposals, voting, and timelocked execution.

## What governance controls

- Fee tiers and treasury allocations
- Router and pool allowlists
- Verifier factory additions and circuit promotions
- Privacy entry policy (e.g. public shield enablement)
- Module authorizations on the shielded token

## Voting

Token-weighted proposals with quorum and delay windows. Shielded tally modes apply where deployed — vote payloads can remain private while the tally is verifiable on-chain.

## Timelock

Sensitive upgrades sit behind a delay so the community can react before execution. Emergency pause paths, if any, are documented in release notes — not bypassable from the app UI.

## Treasury

Ecosystem allocations and LP incentives flow through governance-controlled treasuries. Bond auctions and liquidity mining epochs are contract-scheduled, not discretionary spreadsheet ops.

## Participation

Hold AGS (when circulating) and use the [Governance module](https://app.aegisprotocol.org/governance). Follow [community channels](../resources/community) for proposal announcements.
